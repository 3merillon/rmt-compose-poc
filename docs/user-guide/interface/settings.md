# Settings

Open the gear in the top bar. The panel floats over the score. Drag its header out of the way and keep listening while you change a control. Click the gear or the panel’s × to close it. Each change applies immediately and is saved for this browser and site.

## Appearance

![Appearance settings for theme, note geometry, detail cutoff, envelope display and release tails](/img/settings-appearance.png)

**Reduce detail above** counts notes in the current view. Above the default of **1,536 visible notes**, the selected or hovered note keeps its controls and labels where they fit. Zoom in on a phrase to bring back detail across the view. You can enter a different threshold from 1 to 500,000.

**Never** disables the number box and keeps ordinary detail wherever it fits at the current zoom. The panel warns that choosing Never can slow down panning, zooming and playback when many notes are visible. Clear it to use the number again.

Choose a theme before customising its colours and note shapes. Selecting a preset applies its geometry and clears previous colour overrides. **Envelope inside notes** draws attack and release on a loudness scale. **Tails after the note’s end** shows what rings on after note-off. The written end remains the point you grab or snap to. [Themes and appearance](/user-guide/interface/themes) · [Amplitude, attack and release](/user-guide/playback/instruments)

## Arrows

![Arrows tab with a ratio, cents readout and interval choices](/img/settings-arrows.png)

The ▲ and ▼ controls on a note transpose it. The default is an octave. Choose a ratio, such as `3/2`, to move by a fifth. In **reciprocal** mode the opposite arrow reverses the interval. Choose **independent** to set it separately. [Transposing with arrows](/user-guide/notes/transposing)

## Audio

![Audio settings for master volume, default instrument, voice limit, loop transposition and tempered playback](/img/settings-audio.png)

Set the default voice for a score that leaves the instrument open. Turn **Play tempered (12-TET)** on and replay a chord to compare its written tuning with the nearest twelve equal steps from the BaseNote. The expressions in the score stay intact.

Use this tab for master volume, the voice limit, reverb, stereo spread and limiting. **Keep within an octave** controls how a transposing loop behaves over successive passes. **Tempo range** sets the span of the live tempo slider in the module bar. [Audio and reverb](/user-guide/playback/audio) · [Instruments and players](/user-guide/playback/instruments) · [Tempo and looping](/user-guide/playback/transport)

## Library

![Library tab showing icon size and cents display](/img/settings-library.png)

Increase **Icon size** if tiles are hard to hit with a finger. **Show cents** adds the size in cents to interval tiles. Both changes apply as you work. [Using the module bar](/user-guide/interface/module-bar)

## Scale

![Scale tab showing separate time and pitch controls and their limits](/img/settings-scale.png)

Increase **Horizontal (time)** to spread entrances apart. Increase **Vertical (pitch)** to separate nearby pitches. These are the same X and Y controls as the sliders at the bottom left of the workspace. Neither adjustment changes the score or its sound.

Both start at **1**. The default X range is **0.3–2** and Y is **0.3–5**. In this tab you can enter exact scale values and widen either range within **0.001–1,000**. Narrowing a range brings the current value inside it. The settings, including your chosen scales, survive a reload. [Workspace](/user-guide/interface/workspace#scale-controls-density)

## Resetting

**Reset this tab** and **Reset all** ask for confirmation. **Reset colors to theme** is a narrower reset that preserves the note geometry. You can also set an individual control back by hand. Score Undo covers edits to the composition. Downloaded scores travel independently of these browser preferences.
