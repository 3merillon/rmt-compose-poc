# Composition workflows

- [Reading the canon and its cadences](/tutorials/workflows/canon-and-cadences). Trace three pitches in bar 5, then follow four voices from a dominant seventh to the tonic.
- [Building a module library](/tutorials/workflows/module-library). Copy a selection into Custom, drop it into another passage and export the library layout.
- [Exploring intervals](/tutorials/workflows/intervals). Hear two tones together and compare their relationship with a second interval.
- [Microtonal experiments](/tutorials/workflows/microtonal-experiments). Compare equal divisions and just intervals with a repeating passage.

To return to the original piece between experiments, [restore the startup canon](/user-guide/modules/loading-modules#reset-default-module).
